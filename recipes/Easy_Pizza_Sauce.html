Easy Pizza Sauce

← Sauces

Ingredients

  • 2 (6 ounce) cans tomato paste
  • 2 cloves minced garlic
  • 3 tablespoons dried parsley flakes
  • 4 teaspoons dried onion flakes
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil
  • 2 cups water

Instructions

  • Combine tomato paste, garlic, parsley flakes, onion, oregano, basil and water in 2 quart saucepan.
  • Cook over medium high heat until mixture boils.
  • Reduce heat to low and simmer for 10 minutes.
  • Cool a little and spread on your pizza crust and proceed with the remainder of your toppings.
