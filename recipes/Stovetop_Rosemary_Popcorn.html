Stovetop Rosemary Popcorn

← Snacks

Ingredients

  • 3 tablespoons oil (olive, peanut, etc.)
  • ½ cup popcorn
  • Kosher salt
  • Rosemary

Instructions

  • Preheat oil in the saucepan and add rosemary and salt.
  • Place a kernel or two in the saucepan as the “test” piece, when they pop add the remaining popcorn.
  • Season with salt and enjoy

Notes

** **You can also use a heavy-bottomed bowl with foil on top instead of a saucepan
