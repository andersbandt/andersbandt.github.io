Cold Brew Coffee

← Drinks

Ingredients

  • 1 cup whole coffee beans
  • 4 cups water

Instructions

  • Grind coffee beans coarsely. This makes it so the grounds don’t cloud the water.
  • Add coffee.
  • Let sit for 12 hours or so at room temperature. Alternatively, let sit in the fridge for about 24 hours
  • Strain out coffee grounds.

Notes

In all honesty, the times seem to be slightly arbitrary. I haven’t done any thorough testing on the times.
