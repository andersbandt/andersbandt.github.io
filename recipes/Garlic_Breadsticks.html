Garlic Breadsticks

← Breads and Doughs > Bread Appetizers and Sides

Ingredients

For pizza dough

  • About 1 lb. pizza dough (see pizza dough recipe)
  • 3 teaspoons garlic powder
  • 3 tablespoons parmesan cheese
  • 2 tablespoons sugar

For buttery goodness topping

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 3 cloves garlic, chopped

Instructions

  • Make pizza dough as directed, but make sure to add other ingredients listed.
  • Divide dough into 8-10 portions. Shape each into a ball, and then roll into rope shape..
  • Place on greased baking sheets and bake at 350 degrees F for 18-22 minutes.
  • Meanwhile, make buttery topping. Heat garlic cloves in oil for 1-2 minutes or until just starting to brown, then add in last amount of butter.
  • When breadsticks are done, remove from oven and coat with prepared topping.

Notes

You can alter the olive oil/butter ratio so how you see fit.
