Measuring sizing standards

Teaspoons = teaspoon Tablespoons = tablespoon Pounds = lb. or lbs. Ounces = ounces

Amount standards

No small fractions. 1/4 as opposed to ¼

Description standards

Items should be listed first along with how they are prepared. Example: Red onion, chopped

As opposed to: Chopped red onion

Ingredients that are optional should be denoted with parentheses. Example: Red onion, chopped (optional) Or 1 lb. chicken (optional)

Instruction standards

Refrain from mentioning item AMOUNTS in the instructions, these should just be referenced in the ingredients list. Exceptions can be made when an item is split between two parts (for example butter for dough and butter for frying or something)
