Ingredients

1 protein source

  • Chickpeas
  • Tofu
  • Meat (chicken)
  • Sashimi style fish

2 cups cooked grain

  • Brown rice
  • Quinoa
  • Couscous

2 cups mixed vegetables

  • Bell peppers
  • Broccoli
  • Carrots
  • Cucumber
  • Zucchini

2 cups extraneous vegetable

  • Red cabbage
  • Potatoes
  • Sushi ginger
  • Pickled red onion
  • Sweet potatoes

1 sauce

  • Hummus
  • Tzatziki

A lil’ spice (optional)

  • Jalapeno
  • Hot sauce

A lil’ sweet (optional)

  • Lime slices
  • Chopped herbs (parsley, basil, etc.)

Instructions

  • Cook potatoes and mixed vegetable on a lined baking sheet for 20-25 minutes
  • Assemble Buddha bowls
