Guacamole Fresca

← Appetizers

Ingredients

  • 3 avocados, halved and pitted
  • 1/4 cup plum tomatoes, seeded and finely diced
  • 1/4 red onion, finely diced
  • 1 jalapeno, seeded and finely diced
  • 2 tablespoons minced cilantro]
  • Juice of 1/2 lime
  • 1/2 teaspoon fine sea salt

Instructions

  • In a medium bowl, mash the avocados. Fold in the remaining ingredients. Taste and adjust the seasonings as needed.
