Steak Sandwich

← Sandwiches

Prep Time: xx minutes  |  Cook Time: xx minutes  |  Serves: 3-4 people

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup mayonnaise , preferably whole egg
  • 1 large garlic clove , minced
  • 3 large onions , peeled and finely sliced (brown, white or yellow) (see note)
  • 40 g / 3 tbsp butter
  • 2 tablespoon brown sugar
  • 1 tablespoon balsamic vinegar
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt & pepper
  • 600 - 700 g / 1.2 - 1.4 lb grilling beef steak
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
  • 60 g / 2 oz rocket /arugula or other lettuce of choice
  • Mustard (I use American)
  • Some good ass bread

Instructions

Garlic aioli

  • Mix mayonnaise and garlic together. Set aside for 30 minutes for the flavor to develop

Caramelized Onion

  • Melt butter in a skillet over medium low heat. Add onions and stir to coat in butter. Place lid on (or place a baking tray over skillet) and leave for 20 minutes, stirring once or twice. Remove lid then cook for a further 20 minutes, stirring every now and then, until onion is golden (increase heat slightly if the color is not changing). Add sugar, vinegar, salt and pepper. Cook for a further 10 minutes until jammy. Remove from heat and keep warm.

Sandwich

  • Take beef out of fridge 20 minutes before cooking. Season generously with salt and pepper.
  • Heat oil in a skillet over high heat (or heat BBQ). Cook steak to your liking - if using a secondary cut like Bavette, skirt or flat iron, it's best medium rare.
  • Transfer beef to a plate, cover loosely with foil and rest for 5 - 10 minutes. Then slice thinly against the grain.
  • Cut your good ass bread and toast lightly.
  • Spread the bottom piece generously with garlic aioli. Top with rocket, then tomato slices, beef, caramelized onion. Spread the top piece of bread with mustard then place on top.

Notes

3 onions seems like a lot but properly carmalized onions reduce down a lot
