French Toast

← Breakfast

Ingredients

  • 2 egg
  • 2/3 cup milk
  • 1/4 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 6 pieces of bread
  • Butter (for the pan)

Instructions

  • Beat all the ingredients together besides the bread
  • Soak the bread in the ingredients
  • Fry on a pan with a heaping tablespoon of butter

Notes

All egg whites can be substituted for the egg. Slightly stale bread works best. Slightly stale french roll is king.
