Some good old microwave mac

← Pasta

Ingredients

  • .5 cup elbow macaroni
  • .5 cup water
  • Pinch of salt :)
  • Shredded cheese
  • .25 cup milk +
  • Pepper
  • Paprika??

Instructions

  • Take the macaroni and the water and the salt and microwave it for 2 minutes then stir and then microwave it for 2 minutes more and then maybe 1 minute more then.
  • Add the salt and stir and then add some shredded cheese and ¼ cup of some good milk.
  • Microwave some more and then add the pepper and anything else and then eat
