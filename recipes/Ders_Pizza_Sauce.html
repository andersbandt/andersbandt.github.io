Ders Pizza Sauce

← Pizza

Prep Time: 5 minutes  |  Cook Time: 30 minutes  |  Serves: 6 people

Ingredients

  • Olive oil
  • Half an onion
  • Garlic
  • 1 can of tomato sauce
  • A little water
  • Salt and pepper
  • Spices (red pepper flakes, paprika, cayenne pepper)

Instructions

  • Saute the onion with the garlic, red pepper flakes, oil.
  • After starting to brown, add the tomato sauce
  • Add a little water and bring to boil (or whatever you would call it for a sauce)
  • Let it simmer for 30 minutes. Then it should be good to put on your pizza.

Notes

This makes enough pizza sauce for about 1-2 pizzas. Definitely more than 1 though, so closer to 2. Maybe 1.75 pizzas.

Context

I made this recipe once (October 21st, 2023) and Nolan really liked it so decided to write it down
