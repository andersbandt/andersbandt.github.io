Aperol Spritz

← Drinks

Prep Time: xx minutes  |  Cook Time: xx minutes  |  Serves: x people

Ingredients

  • 3 parts of Prosecco D.O.C.
  • 2 parts of Aperol
  • 1 splash of soda
  • 1 slice of orange

Instructions

  • Place ice cubes in a stemmed balloon glass.
  • Pour 3 parts of Prosecco D.O.C.
  • Follow by pouring 2 parts of Aperol, and 1 ounce of soda.
  • Slice an orange and use it as a garnish.

Notes

Making the perfect Aperol Spritz is as easy as 3-2-1. That’s a ratio of 3 parts Prosecco, 2 parts Aperol, 1 part soda water (or 90ml Prosecco, 60ml Aperol, and 30ml soda). Aperol Spritz is best served with ice, in a wine glass, garnished with a slice of orange, in good company.
