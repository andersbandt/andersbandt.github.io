Cast Iron Pizza

← Pizza

Prep Time: 15 minutes to an hour. Depends on proofing duration and if you have the dough ready.  |  Cook Time: 20 minutes  |  Serves: x people

Ingredients

  • 1 lb. of pizza dough (actually just a guess)

Instructions

  • Stretch out pizza dough into a rough circle slightly bigger than the diameter of your cast iron pan
  • Cover loosely with plastic wrap or a towel and let proof for another 10 minutes, and up to an hour.
  • Preheat oven to 475-500 degrees.
  • Add pizza sauce, and ook the crust briefly on a stovetop for about 5 minutes. This helps with the rising of the dough I think.
  • Add the rest of your toppings and throw in the oven for 18-29 minutes.

Notes

For this recipe, make sure that you don’t overdo it on the dough, as in too much in the cast iron. It is a common theme in your pizzas.
