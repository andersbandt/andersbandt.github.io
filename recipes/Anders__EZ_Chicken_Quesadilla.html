Anders_ EZ Chicken Quesadilla

← Mexican

Ingredients

  • Two tortillas or one
  • Chicken
  • Cheese
  • Onion
  • Red Pepper
  • Paprika (optional)
  • Oregano (optional)
  • Chili powder (optional)

Instructions

  • Cook the chicken and make sure it’s chopped up into bite size pieces
  • Add the vegetables and cook them when the chicken is almost done, maybe about 2 minutes.
  • Pour the mixture onto a tortilla and place it back on the skillet. Add cheese on top of the mixture and then put a tortilla on top of that.
  • Cook about 1.5 minutes per side. It helps a lot when you cook the chicken tortilla with a cover on top of the skillet because it keeps a lot of the heat in the skillet.
  • Pretty good with guacamole
