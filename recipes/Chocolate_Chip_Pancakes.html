Chocolate Chip Pancakes

← Breakfast > Pancakes

Ingredients

  • 1 1⁄4 cups flour
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1⁄4 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • 1⁄4 teaspoon salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 4 tablespoons melted butter
  • 3⁄4 teaspoon vanilla
  • 1⁄3 cup chocolate chips

Instructions

  • Preheat skillet.
  • Combine flour, sugar, cinnamon, baking powder and salt in a large bowl.
  • Mix together wet ingredients (eggs, milk, butter, vanilla) and beat into dry mixture until smooth.
  • Fold in chocolate chips.
  • Pour or spoon batter into fry pan in desired quantity.
  • Flip when top begins to bubble, then cook a minute more.
