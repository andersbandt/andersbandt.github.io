Green Goddess Sauce

← Sauces

Prep Time: 5 minutes  |  Serves: 4 people

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh cilantro or dill
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh parsley
  • 1 teaspoon chopped garlic
  • 1 1/2 cups plant-based mayo (or regular mayo if you’re about it)
  • 2 tablespoons horseradish
  • 1 1/2 tablespoons whole-grain mustard
  • 1 tablespoon capers
  • 1 1/2 tablespoons water

Instructions

  • Combine ingredients in a blender, and process until smooth. Simple as that!
