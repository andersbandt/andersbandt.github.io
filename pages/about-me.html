About Me

My name is Anders Bandt. I graduated from the University of Wisconsin-Madison with degrees in Electrical Engineering and Mathematics, and I'm now working as an electrical engineer. I'm passionate about hardware design, embedded systems, and writing code to make things work.

Some of my other interests include hydroponics, wearable technology, IoT devices, and light/audio design.

Outside of work I spend a lot of time outdoors. I'm an avid runner and have completed several marathons and triathlons, including the Madison Half Ironman. I love hiking, camping, and exploring new places. Recently I converted a van and lived out of it for a summer, which was one of the best experiences of my life.

I also really enjoy cooking - if you haven't already, check out the recipes section of this site.
