types of environments

Really there are two main environments: who we talk to? what we do, where we are. Who what where.

When is right mother fucking now ==integrate this thought more elegantly==

For me I really think about this as your friends, workplace, and where you live.

Friends shape us. We learn things from them, do the same activities, laugh at the same jokes. We tend to share the same values which has rippling impacts on many avenues of life.

We also choose our friends. Maybe you have a couple friends you don't feel like you chose but by and large we don't really hang out with people we don't want to ==clean up previous thought==

Workplace is also important as it's where we spend a majority of waking hours. I am not a believer that our workplace fundamentally changes our character (like friends do) but nonetheless it is a key piece of our environment.

The one I'm wrestling with right now is the third one, where you live. Because in a big way that shapes the who and what of your life. If you are closer to certain people they will be a bigger part of your environment. Certain jobs and hobbies (what you do) are 100% shaped by where you live.

I set out on this van life journey to kind of answer the what's next in my life. And beyond the seeing many cities and people around the country, it's a perfect example of why environment matters so much.
