Scope: this document will be a writing of what I want out of future life after a pit stop in MN awoke some stressful feelings about the trajectory of my life

my focuses

focus 1: start a hardware company

I mean I just have to start my own electronics company. It's the only path that will bring me total fulfillment. Truly. Hiking in Colorado is what cemented that in my brain for me. And when I was hiking in Yoho that's when I thought the same things!

focus 2: relationship building

This is mainly for finding a life parter but should MOST DEFINITELY apply to friendships as well. For the friendship side this includes

  • rekindling past friendships that I value and maintaining contact
  • forging new friendships

focus 3: new experiences

I can't just move back to Minnesota and cut off new experiences. It's too invigorating not to. Can they be compatible? Moving back and no new experiences? I think no. I can try new hobbies, go to more concerts, go out to eat more. Go hard on another hobby outside building like cooking.

focus 4: home ownership

It's weird that this urge has come up so quickly in the past year but I think it is something that I value. I dream of having space to have all my things out and have a workshop. I think of my mini electronics station in Dallas and how much happiness that brought me!

focus 5: health and wellness

Really wherever I live I do want to deep dive into training for something epic again. Whether that's qualifying for Boston, Ironman, knocking out more marathon states (I honestly forgot about that goal)
